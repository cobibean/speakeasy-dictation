SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their original license texts are included here:

- `fonts/OFL-Manrope.txt`
- `fonts/OFL-Newsreader.txt`
- `fonts/OFL-Caveat.txt`

not change the repository's AGPL-3.0-or-later software license.

In the desktop source export, the brand TTFs are in
`src/renderer/assets/brand/` and these notices remain in `docs/brand-kit/fonts/`.
The Newsreader notice also covers renderer Newsreader WOFF2 files.
